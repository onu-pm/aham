# Delete your Aham account

You can delete your Aham account, and all the data stored with it, at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open **Aham** and tap **Me** in the bottom bar.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account**.
3. Tap **Delete my account**, then **Yes, delete everything**.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

## Without the app

Email **thepomonu@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my Aham account"**, from the email address you use to sign in to Aham. We'll delete your account within **7 days** and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: email address, name and sign-in details
- Everything you added: habits, plans and tasks, to-do lists, and settings

Nothing is kept afterwards. Backup files you saved yourself (for example to Google Drive) are under your control, and you can delete them there.
