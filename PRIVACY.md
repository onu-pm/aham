# Aham Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Aham is an app for planning your days, building habits and keeping simple to-do lists. This policy explains what information Aham collects, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- **You sign in with an account** (Google or email and password), so your data is kept safe and follows you to a new phone.
- **We store only what you put into Aham**, plus your email address and name, to run the app.
- **No ads, no tracking, no selling.** We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
- **You can delete your account and all your data at any time** from inside the app.

## What we collect

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | To create your account and let you sign in. Also used to send sign-in, confirmation and password reset emails. |
| **Name** (optional) | To greet you in the app. If you sign in with Google, we receive the name on your Google account. |
| **Password** (email sign-in only) | To sign you in. It is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider. We can never see it. |
| **What you add to Aham**: habits, plans and tasks (with dates, times and steps), to-do lists, your avatar and settings | To show them to you and keep them in sync across your devices. |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers for advertising, or browsing history.

## The weekly league (optional)

If you choose **Join** on the Weekly league in Grow, other Aham members who have also joined can see your **first name, avatar and the points you earned this week**. Nothing else is shown: not your goals, tasks, plans or email. You can **Leave** at any time, which removes your entry straight away.

## Where it's stored

Your account and app data are stored with **Supabase**, our database and sign-in provider, on secure servers. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). A copy is also kept in the app's private storage on your phone, so Aham works offline.

Only you can access your data: every request is checked against your signed-in account. We access stored data only if needed to fix a problem you report, or where the law requires it.

## Speaking instead of typing

When you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech recognition service (on most Android phones, provided by Google) turns your voice into text. Aham receives only the resulting text, which it handles like anything you type. **Aham does not record or store audio.** The speech service is covered by its provider's privacy policy.

## Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone using Android notifications. The times are part of your plans and habits, stored as described above.

## Backups

If you choose **Back up** in the Me screen, Aham creates a file with your data and opens your phone's share options. Where that file goes (for example Google Drive, email or your Files app) is your choice, and is then covered by that service's own privacy policy.

## Optional AI task breakdown

Some versions of Aham can use an AI service to suggest small steps for a task or habit. **Only when you tap "Break into micro-tasks" or "Break into a tiny daily routine"**, and only if this feature is enabled, the title of that one task or habit is sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's Claude API to generate steps. Nothing else is sent. When the feature is not enabled, Aham uses built-in step templates and nothing is sent.

## Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. We use these service providers only to run Aham:

- **Supabase**: account sign-in and data storage
- **Google**: only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are

## Deleting your account and data

- **In the app:** go to **Me → Account → Delete account**. Your account and all of its data are permanently deleted straight away.
- **Without the app:** follow the steps at [Delete your Aham account](https://github.com/onu-pm/aham/blob/main/DELETE_ACCOUNT.md), or email **thepomonu@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with. We delete the account and all its data within 7 days.

Signing out removes your data from that phone. It stays in your account until you delete it.

## Health and wellbeing

Aham supports routines and planning. It is **not a medical device** and does not diagnose, treat or replace professional care.

## Children

Aham is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the app's listing.
