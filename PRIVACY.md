# Aham Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Aham is a habit, planning and mood app designed to be private by default. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Aham Android app.

## The short version

- **Your data stays on your phone.** Habits, tasks, goals, moods and Mind entries are stored only on your device.
- **No account, no ads, no tracking.** Aham does not ask you to sign up, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.
- **We never sell or share your data.**

## What Aham stores, and where

Everything you enter (habits, goals, tasks and their steps, reminder times, mood check-ins, Mind entries, your name and avatar, and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you uninstall Aham or clear its data, this information is deleted from your device. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

## Backups

If you choose **Back up** in the Me screen, Aham creates a file containing your data and opens your phone's share options. Where that file goes (for example Google Drive, email or your Files app) is your choice, and is then covered by that service's own privacy policy. **Restore** reads a backup file you pick and replaces the data on your device.

## Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Aham asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your device. No reminder information leaves your phone.

## Optional AI task breakdown

Some versions of Aham can use an AI service to suggest small steps for a task or habit. **Only when you tap "Break into micro-tasks" or "Break into a tiny daily routine"**, and only if this feature is enabled, the title of that one task or habit (for example "Clean my room") is sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's Claude API to generate steps. Nothing else is sent: not your other items, your moods, your Mind entries or your name. When the feature is not enabled, Aham uses built-in step templates and nothing is sent.

## Health and wellbeing

Aham supports routines, focus and self-reflection. It is **not a medical device** and does not diagnose, treat or replace professional care. If you are struggling, please reach out to a medical professional or a local support line.

## Children

Aham is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the app's listing.
