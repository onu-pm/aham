# Aham tester feedback form

A short Google Form for closed-test testers. Each question collects data needed for Google Play's production application ("Apply for production"), shown in the mapping at the bottom.

**How to make it:** go to forms.google.com, start a Blank form, and add the questions below. Then:
- In **Settings → Responses**, turn **Collect email addresses** off, so testers don't need to sign in.
- In **Responses**, click **Link to Sheets**.

Send the form twice: on **day 7** and on **day 13** of the test. Two rounds show Google that testers kept using the app and that you acted on what they said.

---

## Form

**Title:** Aham: tester feedback 💜
**Description:** Thanks for testing Aham! This takes about 2 minutes. Honest answers help most, including "this was confusing".

1. **Your name** (Short answer, optional)

2. **Which phone do you use?** (Short answer)
   _e.g. Redmi Note 9 Pro, Samsung A54_

3. **How often did you open Aham?** (Multiple choice)
   - Every day
   - A few times a week
   - About once a week
   - Only once or twice

4. **Which parts did you try?** (Checkboxes)
   - Home: "Just this one thing" and today's summary
   - Grow: added a habit or goal
   - Grow: ticked a habit off on the calendar
   - Broke a habit or task into tiny steps
   - Plan: added a task for a day
   - Set a reminder
   - Mind: mood check-in
   - Mind: wrote an entry
   - Me: changed text size or name
   - Me: backup or restore

5. **Did reminders arrive on time?** (Multiple choice)
   - Yes, every time
   - Sometimes
   - No
   - I didn't set any

6. **How easy was it to add something new?** (Linear scale, 1 = hard, 5 = very easy)

7. **Was anything broken, slow or confusing?** (Paragraph)
   _Tell me what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots are welcome on WhatsApp._

8. **What did you like most?** (Paragraph)

9. **What's one thing you'd change or add?** (Paragraph)

10. **Would you keep using Aham after the test?** (Multiple choice)
    - Yes
    - Maybe
    - No

11. **Who do you think would find Aham most useful?** (Short answer, optional)

## How the answers map to Google's production questions

| Google asks | Use |
|---|---|
| How easy was it to recruit testers? | Your own experience: how many you invited and how many opted in |
| Did testers use all available features? | Q4, counting how many testers ticked each feature |
| Did usage match expected production behaviour? Any differences? | Q3 (how often), Q4 (features), Q2 (device range) |
| Summary of feedback and how you collected it | Q6–Q9 themes, plus "collected via a Google Form on days 7 and 13, WhatsApp messages, email and Play testing feedback" |
| Target audience (be specific) | Your own answer, with Q11 as supporting evidence |
| Value proposition | Q8 (what testers liked), in your own words |
| Estimated first-year installs | Your own estimate |
| Changes made because of the closed test | The change log below |
| How you decided the app was ready | Q5 (reminders reliable), Q7 (no open bugs), Q10 (would keep using) |
